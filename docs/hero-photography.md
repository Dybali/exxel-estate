# Homepage photograph

The homepage uses a real photograph from Antalya, Turkey, by Vlada Karpovich.

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/villa-with-a-scenic-view-of-the-sea-7902904/
- License: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Asset: `public/antalya-mediterranean.webp`, mirrored under `apps/web/public/`.

The source photograph was resized to 1600 pixels wide and converted to WebP at quality 72, without generative editing (374,148 bytes, compared with the downloaded 1,067,930-byte JPEG). Desktop and mobile background positions preserve the villa and Mediterranean view. The existing homepage layout and overlay remain in place. The visible photo credit links to the source.
